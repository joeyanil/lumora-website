Lumi K2

A school management system that brings administration, student records, attendance, and parent–teacher communication into one calm, reliable place.

CategoryEducation AI
Primary languageTypeScript
Last updated3 days ago
RepositoryPrivate

Overview

Lumi K2 is the founding product of the Lumi family — a school management system built around one observation: most school software is designed for the district office, not the front-desk staff and teachers who actually touch it every day. K2 is built the other way around, starting from the daily workflow and working up to the reporting layer.

What it does

  • Student records — enrolment, guardians, health notes, and academic history in one profile.
  • Attendance — fast daily marking with automatic flags for patterns that need a human look.
  • Communication — parent–teacher messaging and notices, including translation support for multilingual school communities.
  • Scheduling — timetables and room assignments that survive a mid-term change without a spreadsheet rebuild.

Why it's under Education AI

Lumi K2 is both a product and a research vehicle. Every real deployment feeds Lumora's Education AI research area — what breaks under real classroom conditions, what teachers actually use versus what they route around, and where AI assistance genuinely reduces admin load rather than adding another dashboard to check.
