01 / 04
Planned

Autonomous AI

Led by Yugen — Lumora's working name for agent research that plans multi-step tasks, calls tools, and can account for its own decisions afterward.

This programme isn't running production systems yet. Today it's early experimentation: task decomposition, tool-use reliability, and the evaluation methodology needed before an autonomous agent earns a place in anything user-facing. We'd rather tell you it's planned than dress up a prototype as a product.

02 / 04
Active

AI Systems

The reliability layer behind everything Lumora ships: evaluation harnesses, safety checks, and the deployment engineering that keeps the Lumi family behaving the way we said it would.

This is the least visible research area and arguably the most active one — it doesn't ship a product of its own, but nothing else ships without it. Work here includes benchmark design, regression testing across model updates, and observability for production services.

03 / 04
Active

Education AI

Anchored by Lumi K2 — learning and school-administration systems designed against real classrooms and real school operations, not idealised ones.

The starting hypothesis: most "AI for education" tools are built for a demo, not a Monday morning at an understaffed school office. This programme exists to close that gap — starting with attendance, records, and communication, and expanding as Lumi K2 earns more trust in real deployments.

Planned

Cybersecurity AI

AI-assisted defence — detection, triage, and response — aimed at the parts of security work that are mostly pattern-matching and always in short supply.

Early and planned: right now this means threat-triage prototyping and reading the literature closely enough to know which parts of the pipeline actually benefit from AI assistance versus where it introduces new risk. No production claims here yet, by design.
