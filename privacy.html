Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What we collect

The only personal information Lumora collects through this website is what you submit through the contact form: your name, email address, a topic, and your message. We don't use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics that build a profile of you as an individual.

How we use it

Your contact form submission is used for exactly one purpose: to read and reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list, share it with third parties, or use it for anything beyond the conversation you started.

How long we keep it

We retain contact messages until you ask us to delete them, or until we've resolved the matter you wrote in about and no longer need the record. There's no fixed retention schedule beyond that.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete any information we hold about you at any time. Email dev.lumora@gmail.com and we'll handle it directly — there's no automated process to navigate.

Aggregate analytics

We may look at anonymous, aggregate visit statistics (how many people visited, which pages, which country) to understand whether the site is being used. This data isn't tied to your identity and isn't collected through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
