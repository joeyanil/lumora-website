About Lumora

Founded on a simple idea: build what you can measure.

An independent AI research and development practice, based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

Where the name comes from

Lumora is built from two roots: lum — light, clarity, understanding — and ora — the horizon, a beginning, a possibility. Put together, it describes what we're trying to do: bring difficult problems into focus, at the exact point where certainty runs out and research begins.

The visual identity carries the same idea. Our mark is a horizon line rising into a single point of first light — the Rising Mark. Every page on this site opens in that same pre-dawn state before settling into daylight, because that's the actual shape of the work: dark, uncertain, then clear.

The short story

Lumora was founded in Addis Ababa as a solo research and development practice. The starting bet was narrow and specific: pick real, unglamorous problems — starting with how schools run day to day — and build AI systems disciplined enough to actually work in them, not just in a demo.

That first system became Lumi K2, the founding member of the Lumi product family. Everything Lumora ships from here carries the Lumi name, and every research area behind it is tracked honestly: active when it's running, planned when it isn't yet.

Research philosophy

Every claim earned.
Every system measured.

We don't publish a research area as "active" unless it's genuinely running, and we don't call something shipped until it's in real use. That standard is slower than a marketing calendar — it's also the only way an independent lab earns trust with no name to trade on.

Evidence over claims

Status labels on this site — active, planned, live, in-progress — describe reality, not ambition.

Build in the open, ship in private

Our engineering happens in private repositories, but every project's purpose, progress, and README are public here.

One name, one standard

Every product carries the Lumi name. That's a promise about quality, not just a naming convention.
