Aegis Sentinel

An early-stage AI-assisted threat-triage prototype for prioritising security alerts under real alert volume.

CategoryCybersecurity AI
Primary languageRust
Last updated1 month ago
RepositoryPrivate

Overview

Aegis Sentinel is an early prototype exploring where AI assistance genuinely helps in security operations — specifically, prioritising alerts when the volume coming in outpaces the analysts reading them.

What it does

  • Alert scoring — ranks incoming alerts by an estimated urgency signal, not just raw severity.
  • Deduplication and correlation — groups related alerts so an analyst sees one incident, not fifteen fragments of it.
  • Analyst feedback loop — every override an analyst makes is captured to recalibrate the scoring model.

Why it's under Cybersecurity AI

This is deliberately kept as a prototype rather than a product. Cybersecurity AI is listed as planned because we'd rather validate the approach carefully — false confidence in a security tool is worse than no tool.

Status

In progress. Prototype stage, evaluated against historical alert data; not deployed against live traffic.
