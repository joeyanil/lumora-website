Security

Security Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue anywhere on this site, email dev.lumora@gmail.com with as much detail as you can share: the page or endpoint affected, the steps to reproduce, and what impact you think it has. We read every message ourselves.

What's in scope

  • This website and its static assets (the pages, scripts, and stylesheets served from this domain).
  • The contact form and its client-side handling.
  • Misconfigured security headers, CSP gaps, or transport (TLS) issues.

What's out of scope

  • Third-party services we merely link to or load fonts from (report those directly to the provider).
  • Denial-of-service testing, automated scanning that generates significant traffic, or social engineering against Lumora or its contacts.
  • Issues that require physical access to a visitor's device.

Our commitment

We'll acknowledge your report, investigate promptly, and keep you updated as we work on a fix. We ask for a reasonable window to resolve confirmed issues before any public disclosure, and we won't pursue legal action against good-faith research that respects the scope above.

Machine-readable contact

This policy is also published at /.well-known/security.txt per RFC 9116.
